# Mortgage Guideline Knowledge Base

## Purchase file minimum documentation
Purchase loans require a signed purchase contract, identity verification, income verification, asset documentation, and appraisal evidence before final underwriting review.

## Refinance file minimum documentation
Refinance loans require income verification, asset documentation, title report, homeowners insurance, and payoff details before final clear-to-close review.

## Self-employed borrower income
Self-employed borrowers usually require two years of personal or business tax returns, current profit and loss support, and verification that income is stable and likely to continue.

## High LTV review
Files with LTV at or above 90 percent should be reviewed for mortgage insurance, investor overlays, pricing impact, and compensating factors.

## Borrower communication
Borrower requests should be concise, specific, friendly, and action-oriented. Avoid internal underwriting jargon and ask for only the next documents needed.
